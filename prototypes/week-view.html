Week view

Five directions for seeing Tree-do a week at a time. All five read the same week (Wed 21 Oct, 11:20) and the same tasks, so tick something in one and the other four follow.

LinesHoursDaysRhythmBoard

1Lines

The map, stretched over seven days. Each list is one line running left to right; each day is a stop showing how much of that list got done. Hover a line to quiet the others.

Leads with
Lists
Best for
Seeing where each list is heading

2Hours

A familiar calendar grid for the few things that have a time, with everything else held in an Anytime tray per day, folded to one chip per list. Open the tray to see the tasks.

Leads with
Time
Best for
Appointments, standups, meds

3Days

A day per row. Every day folds to a strip of tiny lines; today opens into its full timetable with branches. Past days say what carried over instead of showing an overdue pile.

Leads with
Today
Best for
Phones, low-overwhelm scanning

4Rhythm

Every task gets its own little line across the week, so repeats become a pattern you can read at a glance. One-offs show where they started and where they moved to.

Leads with
Repeats
Best for
Habits, weekly review

5Board

Today stays big on the left, the panel you already know. The rest of the week sits beside it as columns to drag one-offs into, with Someday at the end. Repeats fold away.

Leads with
Planning
Best for
Spreading the week out
