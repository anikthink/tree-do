Drag, then set it up

Drag on empty time to make a task as long as you need. As soon as you let go, the task's editor opens next to it so you can name it, pick its track and adjust the time, with quick lengths one tap away. The bar at the top of each day shows how much of it is planned, so you can see when a day gets too full before it happens.
